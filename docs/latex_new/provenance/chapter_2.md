## Interpretation boundary

The study is formative, non-random, and computing-focused. Interview notes
were converted to observation statements without transcript-level coding.
The chapter uses counts for descriptive prioritization within the sample and
does not infer population prevalence. The storyboard and affinity map are
design evidence. They are not implementation or effectiveness results.
