Formatting policy applied to the thesis:

- complete journal articles are rendered as `[J]`; DOI and URL fields were removed from
  the thesis bibliography so that they are not misclassified as online resources;
- conference papers are rendered as `[C]` with a city and publisher; DOI and URL fields
  were removed from the rendered bibliography;
- the arXiv annual report is rendered as `[R/OL]` with publication place, institution,
  publication year, access date, and URL;
- web documentation and product pages are rendered as `[EB/OL]` with a complete ISO
  access date and direct URL;
- journals that publish continuously without an issue number, and publications that use
  article identifiers rather than page ranges, were not given invented issue or page data.
